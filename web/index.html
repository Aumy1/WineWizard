THE PURPOSE

Wine makes more sense with context.

Use the bottle guide for wine from a store. Use the chemistry lab to learn how a model classifies chemical measurements. These are two different ways to understand wine.

Price and alcohol content do not tell us whether a wine is good, authentic, or right for your taste.

A few words worth knowing.

Grape & region
Variety, growing conditions, and winemaking influence style. A grape name alone cannot describe every bottle.
Body & sweetness
Body describes mouthfeel. Sweetness describes perceived sugar; a fruity aroma doesn’t establish sweetness.
Vintage
The year the grapes were harvested, rather than a score or a promise of quality.
